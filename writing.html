A recurrence of a realization regarding a mode of being; That the thought of doing something, mostly anything, must be immediately followed by a rejection of action. An avenue that has been long traveled, reared its head in every context, and elicited immense frustration, anger, and panic resulting in identity crisis and disassociation. The inability to recognize any particular activity or action as enjoyable or to generate excitement preceding it. Such that, to perform any task, I must overcome a substantial barrier to entry. Is that inevitable? There of course have been moments of peace, wherein actions are not only doable or capable of generating enjoyment, but exist only in themselves. Contrary to the typical feeling, of anticipatory anxiety, that robs all actions of their immediacy…

Reading this evening about the complete mapping of the connectome of the fruit fly brain. Not sure if it’s redundant to say ‘complete mapping of the connectome… Anyway it strikes me that studies like this imply very strange incentives. They are racing toward strange conclusions. You have firstly the idea that mapping neural connections will improve our artificial intelligence models; that our ability to model reality will be improved to that of a human mind… That is to say that we aim to bring our machines to life. But secondly, that we are seeking a physical explanation for the mind. Which will arrive at base first, the particle physicists grand unification or the complete mapping of the human connectome (there I go again)? What will understanding our our neural circuitry really tell us about who we are? Millennials cannot wield irony effectively (in the sense that irony is way of speaking about things that are real in a way that does not define them with specificity; that is, an analysis that suggests multidimensionality). They, in a sense, have built the meta-world that many people live in, a world of hyper irony. If there was some past understanding of the world that could be gleaned through irony-colored glasses, we’re well past that now. Instead, these glasses are night vision goggles donned in broad daylight. It is no longer a tool of analysis except to say that the world in which it is deployed remains completely unseeable. Irony has no point, no vantage, and no dimensionality beyond the superficial rejection of any media presented. Despite this, it is a very reasonable reaction, and perhaps the most logical, when there is no confidence in any information being presented. What if you could play all at once the music/sounds that each individual is listening to, for example, on a crowded university campus. You would hear complete disymphony! And perhaps a sonic allusion to the social discord? Because there is the ability for each to impose a ‘personal’ soundtrack on their environment, each decides to pull away from the collective sonic environment; to pull away from conversation (in its many forms), as an example. There is no agreement on what is happening even directly in front of any two people because they have conspired to the misalignment of sensory information… Another form of this is that we are no longer synchronized in our consumption of media; We are not all tuning into the 8 o’clock news. The recording, the ‘clipification’ of media in combination with algorithmic social media (which is hardly social.. In fact, this kind of algorithmic targeting is HIGHLY personalized and therefore is, in a sense, the anti-synchronized media) feeds the user constantly with ‘new’ information that is ‘new’ only in a very individual sense. How often has a 7 or 8 year old youtube video suddenly appeared on your feed. (I should say also that you often find comments under such videos that read something like ‘who else is here in 2024,’ which suggests some process of ‘group algorithm.’ Something else that deserves attention…) This is a temporally ‘old’ piece of media that has been presented as new. This happens in a faster, more compressed, subtle way in the short form reels, tiktoks, etc. I could probably push back against myself here in saying that these waves of ‘new’ information are not targeted to the individual, but to some algorithmic grouping where any user is placed in a collection of subgroups, each of which contain some collection of media bites belonging to a sub-culture of creators, but I digress… This desynchronization, firstly, contributes in a temporal dimension to the information-confusion (stated also as information-overwhelm) of the age, where no one person can possibly internalize and make sense of general trends socially or in any other discipline. This confusion would tend to result in the individual giving up on any such general analysis and tending back towards their algorithmic sub-corner of the information space. Secondly, it serves to make conversational grounds highly uneven; that is, how can we talk about any issue (or even triviality) when we have internalized, by way of personalized media, discontiguous chronologies. In the same vein, I must directly address the discontinuities of the COVID pandemic. Though the topic has been utterly exhausted, take for example the its effects on a university organization. Observe a serious loss of organizational identity as two generations are stripped (each class being a generation) of cultural hand-me-downs. This results in leadership that is almost completely alienated from its own history. Major structures were preserved, but the subtly and nuance of social traditions and the quality of the group dynamic were completely rebuilt. Rebuilt being a fairly imprecise choice because, in reality, there were no plans, no maps. The only members with links and or knowledge of the past (and incomplete ones, having only that first year or so to imbibe the culture) are a minority faction, and without a sufficient claim to teaching the culture of the organization, the younger generation, with numbers and the vigor of being new, creates a completely new organization. When we emerge from the den, after a year of isolation, we completely forget ourselves. With a huge region of our memory removed, we have no choice but to place our next brick on top of a non-brick, a floating brick-shaped chunk of nothing, which leaves us staring incredulously at a patchwork structure that makes absolutely no sense, yet appears to remain standing.
